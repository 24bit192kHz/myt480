# 2026-11-01: hibernation drain and Deep Sx (C61)

**Symptom.** Measured by battwatch, every hibernation since 2026-10-06 drew 0.27-0.29 W
(10-28: 97 h took BAT1 from 21.2 to 3.7 Wh at 0.18 W), while a full power-off drew
0.09-0.19 W.

**Cause.** The PCH power-gating policies were all zero under coreboot (PWRMBASE 0xfe000000:
`S3_PWRGATE_POL` 0x28, `S4_PWRGATE_POL` 0x2c, `S5_PWRGATE_POL` 0x30, `DSX_CFG` 0x34), so
the suspend well stayed powered in S3, S4 and S5. Lenovo's N24ET74P (probe dumps
`stock-full-*/inteltool-p.txt`, on AC and battery alike) sets 0x3, 0x3, 0xc000 and 0x1: Deep
Sx in every sleep state on AC and battery, LAN_WAKE# kept as a wake pin.

**Fix.** Patch 0030 sets the same values in the T480 devicetree (`deep_s3_enable_ac/dc`,
`deep_s5_enable_ac/dc`, which also covers S4, `deep_sx_config = DSX_EN_LAN_WAKE_PIN`).
WAKE# stays disabled through `PCIEXPWAK_DIS`, as before. The ROM differs from C60 only in
the ramstage, the romstage and postcar (devicetree) and the version string.

**Flashed as C61, 2026-11-01 23:36** (FMAP + COREBOOT, one-shot TPM blob, warm reboot).
Checked afterwards: registers 0x3/0x3/0xc000/0x1, firmware time 0.58 s, disk key re-sealed
to the new PCR 2, MX150 present (D3cold with the driver).
- S3 with an RTC wake: coreboot logged `Enabling Deep S3`, `prev_sleep_state 3`,
  `PM1_STS: WAK RTC`, all 24 PCI functions back, watchdog re-armed.
- Hibernation with an RTC wake: `prev_sleep_state 4`, image restored, lid-wake hook set and
  cleared EC 0x01 bit 6, watchdog re-armed.

**Remote end-to-end audit, 2026-11-01 23:40-23:58 (on AC, lid open).**
- 5 S3 cycles with RTC wakes: 5 entries, 5 exits; MX150 back in D3cold, 24 PCI functions,
  TCO watchdog active, Wi-Fi up after each; no Xid.
- Cold boot from Deep S5 by an RTC alarm (`work/coldboot.sh "" 45`): cbmem `prev_sleep_state 5`,
  `PM1_STS: WAK RTC`, MRC cache used, firmware 1.03 s, SSH up 6.5 s after power-on, disk key
  unsealed by the TPM (PCR 2 742BC24B... as after the warm boot), every service of the
  default bundle up.
- The lid-close path itself: `loginctl suspend-then-hibernate` with HibernateDelaySec=60 and a
  temporary hook arming an RTC wake for the S4 phase (both removed afterwards): S3, elogind's
  own wake, hibernate, RTC wake from S4 (`prev_sleep_state 4`), lid-wake hook armed and
  cleared, watchdog disarmed and re-armed, battwatch split it into sleep + hibernate rows.
- MX150: `nvidia-smi` wakes it (P0, PCIe gen 3), back in D3cold seconds later.
- Logs: cbmem only the known lines (no SLIC, CSE disabled); dmesg only known lines (atkbd
  "Failed to deactivate keyboard" on cold boots since October, Bluetooth -16 since October).
- CPU microcode 0xf6 = the newest release (microcode-20260925), in coreboot and the OS.
- Not possible remotely: battery drain (on AC; `charge_behaviour` offers only `auto`, no
  force-discharge), lid/keyboard/power-button wakes, WoL (no cable).
