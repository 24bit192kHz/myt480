# 2026-10-01: MX150 clock offsets

What can be tuned on the MX150 (GP108, 10de:1d10) under Linux with driver 580xx:

| Knob | Available |
|---|---|
| Power limit (`nvidia-smi -pl`) | no: the driver reports a placeholder (5001 W) and no power readings |
| Application or locked clocks | no (GeForce) |
| Voltage | no (not exposed on Linux) |
| Clock offsets | yes, through NVML without an X screen on the GPU: graphics -200..+1200 MHz, memory -2000..+2000 MHz |

On Pascal a positive graphics offset moves the whole voltage/frequency curve up: the same
voltage gives a higher clock. Tools: `firmware/tools/gpu/`.

## Baseline (AC, 300 s at full load, offsets 0)

44.0 fps in the shader-bound test (720p upscaled to 4K with `ewa_lanczossharp`), steady for
the whole run; graphics clock 1733 MHz falling to 1708 MHz as the chip warmed (GPU Boost
steps), memory 3003 MHz, GPU at most 78 C, no throttle reason in any sample (no power cap,
no thermal slowdown). The fan ran at full speed (thermald-t480 goes to full speed above
70 C), so there is no cooling left to add.

## Chosen and installed (AC only)

Graphics +150, memory +1000: two steps below the failure and the knee. Together they
passed a 15-minute soak: 47 chunks, no stall, no Xid, 45.8 fps in the shader test (+4.1 %),
1837 MHz, GPU at most 86 C, no throttle reason. `gpu-power` sets them from
`/etc/gpu-power.conf` after every driver load; on battery the GPU runs at stock clocks.

## Worth knowing

- The GPU and the CPU share the heatpipe. Under a long GPU load the CPU's own sensor
  reached 94 C with the CPU nearly idle, against a 95 C limit. In a game that also loads
  the CPU, the CPU reaches its limit first; the offsets add a few degrees to that.
- An Electron program started while the NVIDIA driver is loaded (here Bitwarden) opens
  `/dev/nvidiactl` through the EGL vendor probing and holds the driver: `gpu-power` cannot
  turn the GPU off until that program exits, also on battery. Starting such programs with
  `__EGL_VENDOR_LIBRARY_FILENAMES=/usr/share/glvnd/egl_vendor.d/50_mesa.json` keeps them off
  the NVIDIA driver.
